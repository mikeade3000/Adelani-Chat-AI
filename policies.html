Privacy Policy

We aim to collect as little as possible and to handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we store

  • Account details: your username, email address, a securely scrambled (hashed) version of your password, when you accepted these policies, your account status (activated, Premium and its end date) and a count of messages sent.
  • Usage counts: how many presentations and live searches were used each day — linked to your username, or to your internet (IP) address if you use the service as a guest — to apply the daily limits.

Your conversations

  • Your chat history is saved in your own browser on your device, not on our server. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • To produce an answer, your message (and any file content or image you attach) is sent through our server to AI model providers via OpenRouter, and — for Nexus — to a web search provider. We do not keep your conversations on our server. These providers process the data under their own privacy policies.
  • Presentation images are generated by an image service (Pollinations) from short descriptions of your slides.

Voice assistant (Ade)

  • Ade uses your browser's built-in speech features. When you use voice commands, your browser's speech service (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari, Microsoft in Edge) converts your speech to text. Microphone use only happens after you allow it.

Emails

  • We use your email address to confirm your registration, tell you when your account is activated, send these policies, and confirm Premium. We don't sell or share your email for marketing.

Your rights

  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your account data at any time (see Contact). Deleting your account removes your account details; chats in your browser can be cleared by you.
  • Please don't share very sensitive personal, medical or financial details in chats.

  • Answers are generated by AI and can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, even when they sound confident. Nexus shows its sources so you can verify facts.
